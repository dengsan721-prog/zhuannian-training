# Training Demo Design

**Goal:** Build a learner-facing demo that turns one Happiness Station family education module into a simple action training loop.

**Audience:** Adult learners in offline family education classes. The demo must look valuable, feel easy, and make the learner complete one observable family action rather than only read teaching content.

**Training Loop:** The demo follows the expected course logic: notice the old reaction, rehearse one new expression, commit to one 24-hour action, then record three feedback lines.

**Scope:** This first demo uses module 1, "我一开口，家人就烦", as the active training path and shows the other 11 modules as locked or upcoming entries. It does not add backend persistence, accounts, payments, teacher dashboards, or AI generation.

**Interface:** The first screen presents the product as "幸福驿站训练器 Demo" with 12 small module cards. The active card opens the training panel. The panel asks for the learner's old reaction, lets them choose a new expression pattern, produces a concrete action card, and finishes with three feedback fields.

**Success Criteria:** A learner can complete the demo in one sitting on desktop or mobile, and the result page shows the self-persuasion frame: "我不是懂了，是做到了".

**Safety Boundary:** The demo repeats that high-risk situations involving violence, threats, abuse, or self-harm should stop training and seek real-world professional help.
